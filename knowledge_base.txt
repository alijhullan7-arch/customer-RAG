SECTION 6: PRODUCT SPECIFIC TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Smart Audio Headphones (NovaSound Pro & NovaSound Air):
- Issue: Headphones won't connect via Bluetooth.
  Solution: Hold the power button for 10 seconds until the LED flashes blue and red to reset Bluetooth pairing memory.
- Issue: Low volume on left or right earbud.
  Solution: Clean the speaker mesh gently with a soft dry cotton swab. Ensure balance sliders are centered in device audio settings.
6.2 Laptops (NovaBook Series):
- Issue: Laptop will not power on.
  Solution: Perform a hard reset by holding the power button down for 15 seconds. Connect the original USB-C charger and let sit for 30 minutes before powering on again.
- Issue: Trackpad non-responsive.
  Solution: Press Function key + F9 (Fn+F9) to toggle the trackpad lock on or off.
6.3 Smart Watches (NovaFit Ultra):
- Issue: Heart rate sensor not tracking accurately.
  Solution: Tighten wrist strap to sit 1 finger-width above wrist bone. Clean the bottom optical sensor array with micro-fiber cloth.
- Issue: Watch battery draining too fast.
  Solution: Disable "Always-On Display" in Settings > Display, and lower background pulse checking frequency to 10 minutes.

SECTION 12: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ SUMMARY)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What should I do if I received the wrong item?
A: Contact support@technova.com within 48 hours of package arrival with photo evidence. We will email a prepaid return shipping label and ship the correct item immediately.

Q: Can I use my laptop while charging?
A: Yes, all NovaBook laptops feature smart battery bypass circuitry preventing overcharging during active use.

Q: Are TechNova earbuds waterproof?
A: NovaSound Pro earbuds carry an IPX7 rating (submersible up to 1 meter for 30 minutes). NovaSound Air carries an IPX4 splash-proof rating.

Q: How do I change the language in the TechNova Connect App?
A: Go to Settings > Profile > Language Preference, select your preferred language, and restart the app.

Q: Does TechNova ship to PO Boxes?
A: Standard shipping via US Postal Service can deliver to PO Boxes. Express and Next-Day Air options require physical street addresses.

Q: What happens if my product arrives physically damaged?
A: Please keep all outer shipping cartons and internal packing materials intact. Photograph the packaging and item damage, then contact support within 3 business days.

Q: Do you offer bulk discounts for corporate purchases?
A: Yes. For orders exceeding 20 units, contact our corporate sales team directly at enterprise@technova.com.
